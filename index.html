Overview

The problem

Penetration testing workflows remain heavily manual and time-consuming, while global attack surfaces expand exponentially creating a dangerous execution gap for security teams.

Our solution

NeuroShell turns a plain-English instruction into safe, validated action. It understands the intent, plans the exact command, runs it in a sandbox, repairs its own errors and ranks the vulnerabilities that matter most.
